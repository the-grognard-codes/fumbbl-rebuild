# Human team v1 sample assets

Copied from `.notes/art-preview/humans-team-v1/sprites` for the standalone pitch
preview. Original manifest, prompts and provenance remain in that source folder.
36x36 transparent PNGs use bottom-center anchors. The ogre is 45x45 and retains
its intentional 25% overhang. Render with nearest-neighbour/pixelated sampling.
Both mock teams share the artwork; gold solid and cyan dashed bases identify sides.
