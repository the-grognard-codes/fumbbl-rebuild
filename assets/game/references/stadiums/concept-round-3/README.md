## Owner notes

- **The pitch must be less busy than in these concepts, for gameplay.** Keep
  the turf's character but reduce dirt patches, flowers, mottling contrast and
  decorative markings on the 26×15 playing surface so players, the ball,
  movement guidance and square highlights read clearly at every camera. The
  apron and stands may keep their full detail.
- This is the third stadium round. Earlier rounds were rejected on visual
  quality; compare work directly against these images.

## Reading the concepts

- All four share one layout: long sidelines with a stand, wall, torches,
  banners and benches on an apron outside the pitch, and a far end wall.
  The **visiting section is smaller** than the home crowd. The long-form request
  states that the revised mockups with expanded sidelines and smaller away
  sections take precedence over its initial concepts.
- The concepts are single painted views. The production work must still meet
  the game's geometry: the unchanged 26×15 pitch and input geometry, all camera
  angles (30/40/50 degrees and overhead), both coach ends, and fixed world
  anchors for every layer.
