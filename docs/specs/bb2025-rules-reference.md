## History, precedence, and limits

The [November 2025 Third Season errata PDF](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_14-11_bloodbowl_faq_errata-ngh7bivuzu-vslz4fw2nm.pdf) was inspected: one page, three rulebook corrections, all retained in May 2026. Retain it as a historical pointer. No later core errata or separate current supplement errata appeared in the checked listing or targeted publisher searches. This does **not** establish an exhaustive archive of every historical file or regional correction. Second Season FAQ files and the listing's 2024 blank draft form are outside this rules register.

Proposed project precedence: applicable latest official errata overrides the base text; official FAQ/commentary resolves covered questions; applicable supplement rules supply their specific scope. Use Blood Bowl Base for lookup and confirm conflicts from first-party sources. Report engine discrepancies separately as implementation findings, with edition, source date, page/section, and observed behavior; engine behavior is not a rules authority. Recheck the official listing before relying on this dated snapshot.
