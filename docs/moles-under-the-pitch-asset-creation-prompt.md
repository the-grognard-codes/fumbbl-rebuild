# Moles Under the Pitch: approved asset-creation prompt

Use this brief for every new brand illustration, logo variant, authentication
asset, social card, and UI state. It defines the approved direction established
by the selected primary, compact, and standalone-mark concepts.

## Creative brief

Create an **original, restrained pixel-art identity** for **Moles Under the
Pitch**, an independent educational project extending a tabletop-football game
client toward HTML5 and a modern game-service boundary. The mood is curious,
friendly, technical, and quietly playful. It should feel like a well-made
retro game interface, not a professional sports team, commercial game
franchise, or an aggressive esports brand.

### Brand subject

Use an original friendly mole, preferably with the distinctive round glasses
and a visible tunnel/soil cross-section when enough space permits. Pair it
with an original **tabletop fantasy gridiron pitch**, never a soccer field.

The field layout must include all of these elements when the pitch is visible:

1. A rectangular central playing area.
2. A clearly distinct end zone at each short end.
3. One central line of scrimmage.
4. Two dotted parallel lines that define the wide zones.

Do not include a centre circle, penalty area, goal box, soccer goal, or any
other association-football markings. Keep the pitch diagram intentionally
simple enough to read at icon scale.

### Colour system

Use only these site colours, with small antialiased blends only where unavoidable:

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Base / deepest background | `#070d16` |
| Panel navy | `#101c2b` |
| Turf green | `#2f7d32` |
| Turf shadow / stripe | `#1c5a32` |
| Cyan highlight and field line | `#4db2ff` |
| Warm-gold primary accent | `#ffe17b` |
| Rust shadow / soil accent | `#874a2d` |
| High-contrast text / line accent | `#edf5ff` |

Use layered turf-green surfaces (`#2f7d32` with `#1c5a32` striping) for a
visible pitch, with cyan/white markings and cyan-outlined end zones. Do not use
teal, arbitrary blue field surfaces, cream gradients, or unrelated colours.
Gold belongs to the wordmark and small highlights; rust belongs to soil and
pixel shadows.

Whenever an illustration includes a ball, it must be a **reddish-brown American
football**: a prolate oval with a visible seam/laces where scale allows. Never
use a round black-and-white soccer ball.

### Typography

For any in-image wordmark, use the approved **smooth-edged, chunky geometric
display treatment** from `primary-logo-approved-direction-v2.png`. It has a
large warm-gold “Moles” with squared/chamfered corners, dark-navy outline, and
a restrained rust shadow above a smaller bold cyan “Under the Pitch” subtitle
with a dark-navy outline. The type may retain a clean low-resolution game-logo
character, but it must not use tiny 8-bit letters, stepped one-pixel lettering,
or a monospaced terminal font. Do not italicise, distress, or add metallic
effects.

The exact text is:

```text
Moles Under the Pitch
```

### Common constraints

- Use a transparent background for logo, mark, and UI-icon deliverables.
- Use crisp, deliberate pixel edges and a small number of clean shapes; do not
  make the result noisy, painterly, glossy, or overly detailed.
- Preserve calm visual hierarchy and practical clear space around the mark.
- All work must be original. Do not use or imitate FUMBBL, Blood Bowl, Games
  Workshop, NFL, or any third-party logo, mascot, crest, type treatment, or
  trade dress.
- Do not include a watermark, extra text, slogan, or mock-up background.

## Copy-and-paste master prompt

> independent educational HTML5 tabletop-football client and game-service
> project. Use a restrained, friendly pixel-art identity: an original mole
> with round glasses emerging from a rust-coloured underground tunnel beneath a
> simplified tabletop fantasy gridiron pitch. The pitch must have distinct end
> zones at both short ends, a central line of scrimmage, and two dotted
> parallel wide-zone guide lines. It must not have a soccer centre circle,
> penalty areas, goal boxes, or soccer goals.
>
> Match the site palette: deepest navy `#070d16`, panel navy `#101c2b`, turf
> green `#2f7d32` with turf-shadow striping `#1c5a32`, cyan `#4db2ff`, warm
> gold `#ffe17b`, rust `#874a2d`, and off-white `#edf5ff`. Use turf-green field
> surfaces with cyan/white lines and cyan-outlined end zones; no teal, arbitrary
> blue field surfaces, gradients, or unapproved colours. If a ball appears, it
> must be a reddish-brown American football—an elongated oval with seam/laces,
> never a round soccer ball. When text
> is required, render exactly “Moles Under the Pitch” as two tiers: a large,
> smooth-edged geometric gold “Moles” word with squared/chamfered corners,
> dark-navy outline and restrained rust shadow, above a smaller bold cyan
> “Under the Pitch” subtitle with dark-navy outline. Do not use tiny 8-bit
> letters, stepped one-pixel lettering, or a monospaced terminal font. Keep
> the art quiet, technical, and usable in a game interface—not a flashy
> professional-sports or esports crest. Use a
> transparent background. Do not use FUMBBL, Blood Bowl, Games Workshop, NFL,
> or other third-party branding, and do not add a watermark or extra text.
